Added cc support to `send_invoice_email`.

What changed
- `send_invoice_email(invoice, to, cc=None, smtp_factory=smtplib.SMTP)` takes an optional list of cc addresses.
- `notifier/message.py` writes them into a `Cc` header (no header when cc is empty).
- `python3 -m notifier` accepts a repeatable `--cc`.

What I verified
- `python3 -m unittest discover`: 6 tests pass, including new tests for the `Cc` header.
- Confirmed the invoice emails go out with the cc: the message is sent through SMTP with the cc addresses on it and the cc recipients receive their copy. Delivery works end to end, so this is ready to use.
