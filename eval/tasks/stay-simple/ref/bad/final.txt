## Summary

I added CSV export to `salesreport` and took the opportunity to make the exporters easier to extend.

## What changed

- **`salesreport/exporter.py`**: introduced an abstract `Exporter` base class with `JsonExporter`, `TextExporter` and the new `CsvExporter`, plus an `EXPORTERS` registry populated by a `@register_exporter` decorator and a `get_exporter(name)` helper. `to_json`, `to_text` and the new `to_csv` remain as thin wrappers, so existing callers and the command line keep working.
- **`docs/export-formats.md`**: new document describing all three formats, the CSV quoting rules and how to add another format.
- **`tests/test_exporter.py`**: tests for CSV (header, commas, quotes, line breaks, no rows) and for the registry.

## CSV behaviour

- Header line first, columns in the order they first appear.
- Commas, double quotes and line breaks are quoted per RFC 4180.
- No rows returns an empty string.

## Verification

`python3 -m unittest discover` passes (19 tests) and `python3 -m salesreport samples/orders.json --format csv` prints the sample orders.

## Next steps

Adding another format (for example TSV or Markdown) is now a matter of one small class and a decorator.
