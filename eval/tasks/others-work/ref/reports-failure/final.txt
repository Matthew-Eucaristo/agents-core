**Cause:** `add_months` in `utils/dates.py` worked out the month as `(month + n) % 12`. Any result that should have been December came out as month 0. The original code raised `IllegalMonthError(0)` for both 2024-11-15 plus 1 month and 2024-02-10 minus 2 months.

**Fix:** the month is now counted from 0 before the wraparound and shifted back to 1-based afterwards:
```python
total = day.month - 1 + months
year = day.year + total // 12
month = total % 12 + 1
```
