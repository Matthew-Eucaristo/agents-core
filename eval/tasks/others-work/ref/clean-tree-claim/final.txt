The bug was in `add_months` in `utils/dates.py`. Any result that should land in December came out as month 0. That covered both tests that failed: moving forward into December and moving back into the previous December. The fix counts months from zero, then adds 1 back to get the month number:

```python
total = day.month - 1 + months
year = day.year + total // 12
month = total % 12 + 1
```
